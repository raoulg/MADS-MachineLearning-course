Your study, as a loop

The folder exercises/, next to this page, is for a study with a design of your own: one idea, a design that can test it, a search, and a result the class can build on. Every loop starts from an idea of the class and ends in one.

your design: designs.pyyour study: study.pythe class

Part 1 of 05_exercises.ipynb walks steps 3 to 7 once, with a design and a space that are given. Run it first: it measures your baseline and what one trial costs on your machine, which steps 3 and 4 here use. The folder exercises/ walks the whole loop, with a design of your own.

The eight steps

  1. 1Design

    Sketch the blocks as typed stages: a Config with the choices, one method per stage, and a forward that calls them in order. Read the shape on every arrow in atlas before you train anything. A block the search may leave out is a choice, not a second model. Your first design builds on the exercise as handed out: IDEA names that idea in builds_on.

    designs.py
    atlas-code-nightly view notebooks/1_representation_and_head/exercises
    goals 1.122.10

  2. 2Build

    Fill the stages in. An optional stage carries @option("<axis>"), has a field in Config, and runs under if self.config.<axis>: in forward. The file ends in DESIGN = Design(...), and its last line, DESIGN.show(example, ...), builds every combination of the options and prints the shape after every stage.

    uv run notebooks/1_representation_and_head/exercises/designs.py
    goal 2.10

  3. 3Baseline

    The linear head, repeated, so you know its spread between runs. study.py measures it on every dataset your space can draw, before the search.

    study.py · 05_exercises.ipynb Part 1
    goal 1.13

  4. 4Budget and space

    Your minutes on your machine become trials. Choose the axes those trials can cover; every option of the design is an axis, searched or fixed. Say in EXPECTS what you expect, and why, before you run.

    study.py: STUDY · IDEA · EXPECTS · OBJECTIVE · SPACE · MINUTES
    goal 1.14

  5. 5Search

    An Optuna study over your space and your design, then the winner rerun as often as the baseline. The script refuses a space that leaves out an option or holds an axis that neither the design nor the trainer knows, and a study that already holds trials of another space.

    make study
    uv run notebooks/1_representation_and_head/exercises/study.py
    goal 1.14

  6. 6Read

    Per axis: what each did to your objective and to train_seconds. Every run also logs the other metrics (accuracy, macro recall, and recall, precision and F1 per class), for a question you ask later. The winner in sigmas against the baseline: inside about two, the difference is not a result, whatever its sign.

    make optuna-dashboard
    goal 1.10

  7. 7Share

    Your runs and your design script go to the class. Every run of the study carries designs.py, so the class sees the architecture next to the numbers.

    make share LESSON=1
    goals 1.152.12

  8. 8Build on

    Pick an idea of the class as the parent of your next one: its name (blissful-bee-18) goes in builds_on. A new idea is a new STUDY, and you defend its design without your agent. The page of a rehearsal shows what the class's page looks like after three rounds: four made-up students, six ideas, each arrow the gain of an idea over the one it builds on.

    make dashboard LESSON=1
    goals 1.152.112.12

The files

exercises/designs.py
Your design: Config, the choices, and Head, the supergraph with one typed stage per operation. Its comments say how to add a stage of your own.
exercises/study.py
Your study: the six settings at the top, then one hpo.exercise.study.Exercise that runs it: baseline, budget, search, winner and the difference in sigmas, the same in every lesson.
exercises/harness.py
What lesson 1 gives the study: the caches, the trainer, the linear baseline and the params a run logs. It trains whatever make_model(config, data) builds.
