Which model is best, if you care about two things?

This model is beaten: another model is faster and scores higher. The Pareto front is the set of models that no other model beats.

■ 04 — pareto fronts

goal 3.93.10

exercise 3.5

The models nothing beats: the Pareto front

A model is on the Pareto front when no other model is at least as good on both and better on one. Best on score alone is one end; which point is best depends on the speed you need.

goal 3.93.113.12

Your study: find your own Pareto front

Test the scaling law on your own designs: within a band of compute you choose, find your own Pareto front, not the highest accuracy or the fastest model. Choose a route through the architectures you know and say why; a small model that wins on speed is a result too. A mixture of experts is allowed, but these vectors are close to linearly separable, which limits what it adds.
