The handed-out baseline, run again by everyone: how far one model’s score moves.

How to read this

Everyone ran the handed-out baseline with the same config: 7 runs of one model. Each averages its own seeds on its own machine, and what is left between them is noise, a spread of 0.21 pt (one standard deviation). The band is the median ± that spread. A bar is one run’s spread over its seeds, smaller than the spread between runs, so a difference on the page carries the spread between runs.

Every finished trial above the handed-out baseline, 31 of 62; the 31 under it are left out. Pick an encoder to draw only its trials. The darker a line, the higher its score.

How to read this

One line is one trial. Drag along an axis to keep a range; drag again elsewhere to clear it.

Each grid crosses two choices of the head; a cell is the median of its trials in points above the baseline, a bold number its row’s best.

How to read this

Grey: under the baseline, or fewer than 3 trials. One row per encoder, because a larger encoder lifts every cell and would hide the head; each row has its own colour scale, from its weakest coloured cell to its best. The trials come from different studies with their own learning rates and epochs, so read a cell as what the class happened to find there.

Score against the compute of training the head, the same on every machine; the line is the cheapest way the class found to each score. Click a point to set it beside the trials of about the same compute. 31 trials under the baseline left out.

How to read this

Compute is about 6 FLOPs per parameter per training row per epoch, from the parameters and epochs every trial logs. The encoder ran once, when the cache was made, and is not in it.

Where these runs come from

153 runs submitted to a rehearsal of the class: four made-up students (a, b, c, d), each study one minute long. 66 of them are earlier executions of the handed-out notebook by someone who ran it again; only the latest one counts, so the page reads 87 runs. Every picture reads accuracy. The runs also log macro_recall, f1 per class (47 classes, as f1/<class>), precision per class (47 classes, as precision/<class>), recall per class (47 classes, as recall/<class>); read one with make dashboard LESSON=1 METRIC=<name>.
